# Resume Book Project

@Neil E Pauli 

The Sponsor Talent Portal connects Longhorn Racing’s corporate sponsors with students interested in internships and full-time opportunities.

Students voluntarily create profiles describing their technical experience and career interests. Sponsors browse redacted résumés within their permitted teams, then request access to individual students’ full résumés. Each student decides which companies can receive their information.

The goal is to create a useful sponsorship benefit while giving students control over employer introductions.

## Value of the proposal

**For students:** Relevant opportunities, a way to demonstrate hands-on experience, and control over disclosure of their personal information.

**For sponsors:** A focused way to discover young talent whose project experience aligns with hiring needs.

**For VC firms:** A single sponsorship supporting participation across several portfolio companies, with separate permissions for each organization.

**For Longhorn Racing:** A tangible sponsorship benefit that supports fundraising and students’ professional development.

Sponsorship does not guarantee interviews, hires, or approval from any individual student.

## Student profiles

Participation is completely optional and does not affect team membership.

Students create an account and select their team—Combustion, Solar, or Electric—and their subsystem. Each team has its own subsystem options, managed by administrators.

Profiles also include:

- **Technical focus:** A descriptive specialty, such as Embedded Software & Controls or Composite Structures & Manufacturing.
- **Skills:** Multiple selections from a standardized list, including tools, programming languages, manufacturing methods, and engineering disciplines.
- **Career interests:** Internship, full-time employment, or both.
- **Startup interest:** Yes, no, or not specified.
- **Education:** Degree program, graduation year, and graduation semester.
- **Project experience:** A brief description of work the student has personally contributed.

These structured fields help employers find relevant candidates without relying on promotional headlines or manually reviewing every résumé.

## Résumé upload and redaction

Students upload their full résumé and use a built-in tool to black out sensitive information, such as their name, email, phone number, address, and personal links.

Before publishing, students preview and confirm the version sponsors will see. The platform maintains:

- **A redacted résumé** for eligible sponsors to browse.
- **A full résumé** that remains private until the student approves a specific organization’s request.

The redacted copy must permanently remove the selected content, underlying text, and identifying metadata. Black boxes must not simply cover recoverable text.

Students can update their profiles, replace their résumés, or withdraw from the portal. They should understand that distinctive project details may still identify them despite redaction.

## Sponsor access and employer filtering

Approved sponsors receive access according to their sponsorship arrangement. Administrators can grant access to one team, several teams, or the entire organization.

Within that scope, employers can filter students by:

- Team and subsystem.
- Selected skills.
- Internship or full-time interest.
- Startup interest.
- Graduation year and semester.

Candidate cards display technical focus, relevant skills, career interests, graduation timing, and project contributions. Personal account information remains hidden until access is approved.

## Student-controlled access requests

An employer interested in a student submits a request identifying its organization, the requesting representative, and the intended role or recruiting purpose.

The student receives an email notification and reviews the request in the portal.

- **Accept:** The full résumé becomes accessible to the approved organization.
- **Decline:** The full résumé remains private.
- **No response:** No additional access is granted.

Approval applies only to the requesting organization and the stated purpose. It does not automatically extend to affiliates, investors, or other companies.

Students can revoke future portal access. Revocation cannot retrieve copies an employer has already obtained.

## VC sponsorship and portfolio invitations

A VC firm can sponsor access for itself and a limited number of portfolio companies.

The VC has its own account and can:

- Browse eligible redacted profiles.
- Request résumé access under its own identity.
- View résumés students have approved for the VC firm.
- Invite portfolio companies up to an administrator-configured limit.
- Track participating companies and pending invitations.

Active portfolio accounts and pending invitations both count toward the company limit. Canceling a pending invitation releases its reserved slot.

Each portfolio company has its own account, permissions, and student approvals. The VC cannot impersonate a portfolio company or view its account.

**Approval for the VC does not grant access to its portfolio companies. Approval for a portfolio company does not grant access to the VC.** Every organization must request permission separately.

## Administrator controls

The admin panel provides a directory showing:

- All company accounts, including direct sponsors and VC-associated companies.
- Each VC account.
- The portfolio companies associated with each VC.
- Pending portfolio invitations and account status.

Selecting a company opens its configurable permissions:

- Enable or pause the account.
- Allow browsing of redacted profiles.
- Allow requests for full résumé access.
- Assign permitted teams or organization-wide coverage.
- Record sponsorship tier.
- Set an access expiration date.

Selecting a VC account opens its own permissions and portfolio-company invitation limit. Changes to the VC’s permissions do not automatically change its portfolio companies’ permissions.

Administrators can review access activity and suspend accounts for violations.

## Confidentiality and accountability

Full résumés may be used only for the approved organization’s internal recruiting purposes, within the scope of the student’s consent.

Sharing outside that organization is prohibited, including forwarding to affiliates, external recruiters, or other companies in a VC’s portfolio. Full résumés must not be placed in a shared portfolio-wide database.

The platform should use verified, named employer accounts, company-specific watermarks, and logs of requests, approvals, revocations, and résumé access.

These measures discourage unauthorized redistribution and support investigation, but cannot completely prevent copying or screenshots.
